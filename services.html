Residential Services

From first-time homebuyers to seasoned investors, we guide every residential transaction with expert oversight and personalized attention.

National Services

Across all 50 states, our team leverages deep knowledge of local laws and regulations, adapting seamlessly to each jurisdiction.

With years of experience handling complex transactions nationwide, we manage every detail, ensuring smooth, reliable closings nationwide.

Closing Services

Our experienced team prepares every document required for a smooth and stress-free closing.

From Buyer and Seller Affidavits to Heirship Affidavits, Deeds, Mortgages, and Discharge of Mortgages, we handle all necessary paperwork to ensure your transaction reaches the closing table efficiently and flawlessly.

Full Escrow Services

We provide comprehensive escrow management, acting as a neutral third party to securely manage documents and hold funds in trust according to the direction of all parties involved.

Ascend Title prepares detailed closing statements and ensures accurate fund distribution in strict accordance with Lender, Purchaser, and Seller instructions.

Complete Transaction Management

From meticulous title searches to surveys, reports, settlements, and recordings, our team oversees every aspect of the title process.

By coordinating each step with precision and expertise, we deliver seamless closings while keeping the entire transaction organized and on track.
