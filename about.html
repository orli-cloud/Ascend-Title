Our Story

Where Smooth
Closings Begin

Industry veterans Sam Parnes and David Berney spent years navigating complex title transactions, building a reputation for skill, reliability, and client dedication. After decades in the title industry, they set out to build a company where that level of commitment would be the standard. Bringing together a select team of top-tier professionals who share the same passion, expertise, and commitment to their clients, Ascend Title was built to deliver an enhanced level of service and a closing experience clients can fully rely on.

We uphold a higher standard, grounded in a philosophy of exceptional client care. Our commitment is to deliver a superior title experience in which the client's needs remain at the center of everything we do.
